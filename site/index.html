AudioX turns a Raspberry Pi into a dedicated audio workstation. Connect USB audio, build your routing, shape it with effects, and trigger sounds from a MIDI controller or the Web UI.

Get AudioX Read the documentation

Open source and actively developed. Raspberry Pi 4 and Pi 5 builds are available; Pi 5 support is untested on hardware.

01 / Connect & route

Make the connections you need

Route capture devices through effects to playback outputs. The browser-based routing desk gives you a visual signal graph and live meters, with USB audio and USB networking for host-to-Pi control.

02 / Shape your sound

Effects in your signal path

Add processing such as gain, distortion, gate, pitch, and reverb. Save effect parameters and output levels, and map MIDI controls to your setup.

03 / Play & perform

A soundboard at your fingertips

Upload sounds, assign MIDI notes, choose playback modes, and configure controller lighting. Control AudioX from its Web UI, MIDI inputs, or the device's touchscreen interface.

04 / Keep your setup

Built to be your workstation

Keep routing, MIDI, effects, and volumes between boots. Back up settings and soundboard files before a fresh install, or install a board-matched firmware update through your browser.

A work in progress, not a finished appliance. AudioX is still experimental. Start with a Stable release when available, use Nightly to try newer changes, and keep a backup before updating. Explore the project or contribute on GitHub.

01 / Fresh install

Start with an SD image

Download the .img.xz for your Pi. Flash it with Raspberry Pi Imager's Use custom option, or choose AudioX in the AudioX Imager fork.

Flashing erases the selected drive. Back up your files first.

02 / Existing device

Update without reflashing

In the AudioX Web UI, open System > Firmware Update. Check for a release, or download the matching .axu here and select it for manual installation.

Your browser needs internet for online downloads. Your Pi does not. Installation validates the archive and reboots the device.

AudioX is a work in progress. Back up your configuration before updating. Report an issue
