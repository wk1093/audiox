About this data. Last 30 UTC receipt dates; updated hourly. Offline reports can arrive later. Idle intervals are skipped, so observations are not total uptime or exact audible duration. No installation IDs, raw reports, exact version strings or per-installation filters are public. With very few participants, even daily totals can reveal individual activity. No minimum population is enforced.

CPU and temperature

Effect adoption

Pi families

Observation distribution, not installation counts.

Firmware families

Feature activity
